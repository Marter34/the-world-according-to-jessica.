Helping people grow

My teaching.

Teaching is about more than getting the right answer. It's about helping someone understand how they got there.

Everyone learns differently

I believe a good teacher is willing to explain something in a different way when the first explanation doesn't work. Asking for help should be encouraged, not punished.

Mistakes are part of learning. Students deserve patience, respect, opportunities to improve, and recognition for the effort they put in.

My classroom philosophy

I want people to feel comfortable asking questions, admitting when they're confused, and trying again.

Progress matters more than perfection. My goal is to help people develop confidence and skills they can carry beyond the classroom.
